How to Play Locked In

1. You will be given a master Host Word (e.g., MARKETPLACE).

2. Find shorter hidden words whose letters appear in consecutive sequence inside the host word (e.g., MARKET, PLACE, ART).

3. Type your answer and click Submit or press Enter. Complete the target number of words before time runs out!
